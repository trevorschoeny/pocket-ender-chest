## 1.0.0

First release.

#### Added

- A slot for your ender chest, above your offhand and on top of the elytra and totem slots. It holds a whole stack, and shift-clicked ender chests go straight in.
- Double-click the slot to turn your inventory into your ender chest, and click the x on its corner to go back.
- With Open Anywhere installed, the double-click opens your ender chest as an Open Anywhere tab instead.
- An Ender Chest tab in Inventory Plus's settings, to turn the mod off or hide the slot.
